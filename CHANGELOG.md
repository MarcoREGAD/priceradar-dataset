# Changelog

Notable changes to the dataset's structure or coverage. Day-to-day price
observations are not listed here — they are the commit history itself.

## 2026-09-06 — first public release

- Published observations from 13 August 2026 onwards.
- Four markets: France, United States, Germany, Spain.
- Seven fields per observation, described in `schema.json`.
- Monthly CSV files per market, plus `latest.csv`.
- Twice-daily automated update.
